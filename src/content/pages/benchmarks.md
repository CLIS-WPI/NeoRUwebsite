## Reading the baseline

CPU use is reported as a median number of CPU cores for the reference O-RU. Package power is measured above idle and includes the O-RU and DU together. It is not an isolated O-RU power measurement.

NeoRU’s goal of running one 20 MHz, 2×2 carrier on about one CPU core is a target. It has not been demonstrated by an end-to-end NeoRU measurement, and no speedup or efficiency comparison is claimed here.

## Evidence and comparability

The baseline measurement method and first reference baseline are complete. The public run record and full measurement report are pending publication. Until those materials are supplied, their identifiers and links remain placeholders.

A NeoRU comparison will be added only after measurement under the same conditions as the reference. Test configuration, measurement scope and relevant limitations will accompany the result.
