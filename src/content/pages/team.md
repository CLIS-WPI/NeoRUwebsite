## Built on earlier work

NeoRU succeeds the lab’s earlier OpenRU work, which demonstrated a working open O-RU on commodity hardware and documented the engineering required. NeoRU carries that work forward with a focus on measured behaviour, explicit interfaces and a small real-time core.
