## Spec-First & Test-First Workflow

NeoRU is developed with explicit contracts and automated verification gates:

1. **Specify the contract:** The architecture RFC and seven interface specifications were completed before implementation.
2. **Write tests first:** Unit tests and the deterministic virtual radio verify behaviour without relying on ad-hoc demonstrations.
3. **Human-approved gates:** AI-assisted development operates under strict human-approved gates with a provenance record for every change.

## Automated CI Gates

The isolated development environment and CI pipeline are complete and run:

- Unit tests
- **AddressSanitizer (ASan)**
- **UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer (UBSan)**
- **ThreadSanitizer (TSan)**
- **AArch64 build under emulation**

Passing CI checks establishes what those checks cover; it does not establish real-time timing or hardware performance.
