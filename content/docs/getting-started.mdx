<Lead>NeoRU is an open, software-defined O-RU: the radio-unit end of an O-RAN split 7.2x fronthaul. It is developed in the CLIS Lab at Worcester Polytechnic Institute and licensed under Apache 2.0.</Lead>

## Why NeoRU exists

Open software for the central and distributed units is mature. Projects such as srsRAN Project, OpenAirInterface and OCUDU give researchers a CU and DU they can read, change and measure. The radio unit is different. Most O-RUs are proprietary, fixed-function boxes, so their timing, their raw I/Q samples and their internal behaviour stay out of view.

NeoRU is an attempt to open that last box: a radio unit whose every stage can be inspected, tested without hardware and measured under stated conditions. The [O-RAN split 7.2x](/NeoRUwebsite/docs/oran-split/) page explains where it sits.

## Reading status labels

Every component in these docs carries one of three labels.

- <Status s="done" /> The milestone is complete on its own terms. It does not mean NeoRU is integrated or measured end to end.
- <Status s="in_progress" /> Under active development. Behaviour and interfaces may still change.
- <Status s="planned" /> Designed or scheduled, but not started. No completion date is announced.
